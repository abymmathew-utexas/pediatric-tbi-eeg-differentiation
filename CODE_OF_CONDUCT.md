# Code of Conduct

This project follows a professional, respectful, and inclusive collaboration standard.

## Our Expectations
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome interdisciplinary perspectives.
- Prioritize patient safety and ethical rigor.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sharing sensitive or restricted data
- Misrepresenting clinical relevance of model outputs

## Enforcement
Project maintainers and mentors may review and address violations according to institutional and platform policies.

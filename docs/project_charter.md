# Project Charter

## Title
AI-Assisted Differentiation of Non-Accidental vs Accidental Traumatic Brain Injury Using Background EEG in Pediatric Patients

## Clinical Motivation
Early differentiation between non-accidental and accidental injury can affect urgent care decisions, safety interventions, and legal pathways. This project investigates whether EEG background patterns can support clinician decision-making.

## Objectives
- Build a reproducible EEG preprocessing and feature-engineering pipeline.
- Train and compare ML and DL classifiers for case differentiation.
- Evaluate performance with clinically meaningful metrics.
- Produce publishable preliminary results for academic dissemination.

## Scope
In scope:
- De-identified EEG data preprocessing and quality control
- Feature engineering and model experimentation
- Internal validation and reporting

Out of scope:
- Deployment for direct clinical decision-making
- Use of identifiable patient information

## Success Criteria
- Documented and reproducible pipeline
- Baseline and improved model benchmarks
- Interpretable error analysis and subgroup review
- Draft manuscript abstract and figure set

## Team and Roles
- Clinical mentors: Pediatric Neurosurgery and Pediatric Neurology
- Trainee contributors: graduate students and residents
- Data science collaborators: internal and cross-institutional partners as available

## Governance
- Follow IRB and institutional approvals
- Maintain strict de-identification and access controls
- Track decisions and analyses in version-controlled artifacts
